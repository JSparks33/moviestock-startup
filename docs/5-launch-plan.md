# Go-To-Market & Launch Checklist
## Marketing & User Acquisition
* **Primary Launch Channel:** Movie enthusiast and cord-cutter communities on Reddit (r/MovieSuggestions, r/cordcutters, and subreddit users for international films) alongside targeted campus out reach to recruit our first 50 early adopters because we know that students want to save money.
* **Value Proposition:** MovieStock eliminates streaming fragmentation by uniting worldwide movies and shows into a single cross-platform portal saving users 2-3 hours of browsing time and cutting streaming costs by over 50%.
